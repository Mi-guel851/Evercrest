# Evercrest Properties — Where Legacies Rise

Official digital flagship for **Evercrest Properties**, designed to connect individuals, families, property owners, investors, and developers with vetted residential, commercial, land, and development opportunities.

## Highlights

- **Twilight Architectural Hero & Overlapping Search Pill**: Inspired by contemporary luxury architectural showcases, featuring live multi-corridor, typology, and investment-band filtering.
- **Signature Sloped-Roofline Property Cards**: Tangent-continuous Bézier roofline masks echoing Evercrest's architectural crest across Houses & Villas, Luxury Apartments, Commercial Spaces, and Land & Development mandates.
- **Interactive Property Dossiers & Shortlist Drawer**: Deep-dive evaluation benchmarks (Title status, projected yield/IRR, 5-year appreciation) plus persistent property bookmarking.
- **Informed Evaluation Studio**: Live Legacy Value & Yield Modeler across 3–20 year horizons with instant multi-currency switching (`USD`, `EUR`, `GBP`, `NGN`).
- **Dual-Track Private Advisory Concierge**: Dedicated workflows for acquiring/investing in property or marketing/developing assets with Evercrest.
